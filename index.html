About Dr. Janny

MEET
DR. JANNY

Dr. Janny Myburg (MBChB, Diploma in Mental Health) is a medical doctor with a passion for making complex health information genuinely accessible.

After years of clinical practice, she recognised a pattern: patients were leaving consultations with results they didn't understand and no practical guidance on what to do about them. Glucose health was a recurring blind spot — one with enormous consequences for energy, weight, mental clarity, and long-term disease risk.

What's My Glucose was built to close that gap — combining medical rigour with real-world practicality, so you can make informed decisions about your own body.
